NETWORM

kernel worm auto-battler

You are the operator. Inject the worm, steer it through the kernel, and let its modules kill every process in the way. Survive 25 levels to get root.

A D or ← → steer | Space start | R reroll | U upgrade shop | C catalogue | P pause | M music on
step 1: injectPress Space to load worm.ko. The worm never stops moving: steer it with A/D or the arrow keys. The kernel's walls hurt the head, so don't crash into the edge.
step 2: surviveYour modules attack on their own; keep them alive. Red shapes are hostile processes: kamikazes, repulsors, snipers and the antivirus daemon every 6 levels. Ramming one hurts both of you; Rammer modules are built for it.
step 3: escalateBetween levels, spend gold at the darkmarket. 3 copies of a module rank it to Lv2, 9 to Lv3. Upgrade the market for rarer tiers, stack classes for bonuses, and run Merchants to farm coins.
